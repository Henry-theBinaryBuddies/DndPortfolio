Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

The test print failed twice in a row in its first few layers, both times from the base coming off the bed, even though a 0.2 mm calibration cube had printed fine. Claude checked the file: its underside is one flat disc 56 mm across, far larger than the 20 mm cube, and a large solid base pulls up at its edges as it cools. In the photo the skirt had come off the bed, the raft was lifted, and the nozzle had torn through the curled plastic. The usual fixes are a clean bed, a brim instead of a raft, levelling over the whole base and a slower first layer. Claude made a 2 mm test disc of just the base to try them in fifteen minutes. But I cut my hand scraping the failed print off the bed, and decided to stop test printing and ship.

So nothing was cut. Every feature ships, and the site says plainly what has not been proven on a print: the mail's rings, the cloak, and relief finer than about 1 mm. The export and print preview warn that ram and Dragonborn horn tips need supports.

Effigy Engine is live at effigy.creaturecauldron.com, on Cloudflare Pages next to Creature Cauldron. It is a static site, with no server, keys or database, and every push to master rebuilds it. Before the first deploy, Claude served the built site locally with the same security headers Pages would send (it may load only its own files, plus the exception Manifold's WebAssembly needs) and checked that it opened, previewed and exported with no errors. After I connected it, I exported a tabletop half-orc from the live site and saved its token, and both came out right.
