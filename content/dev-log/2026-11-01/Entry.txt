Artifact 4 (CouchCrew):

The team plan and branches went up on Oct 29: Adrian took the rooms and chat, John the synced YouTube player, and I took audio, video and screen share. My first version used Daily.co. The server made one Daily room per CouchCrew room so the API key stayed off the browser, and the page had its own grid of video tiles with mute, camera and share-screen buttons, plus a "Join call" step that works for someone with no camera or mic.

It merged, and then Adrian tested it live and Daily ended the call with "account missing payment method". Daily's free minutes are part of its paid plan, so a card is needed on the account even for the free allowance. We did not want a billing account for a class project, so Adrian replaced it with Jitsi Meet's free public server in an iframe. That deleted my server code and tile grid. Mic, camera and screen share still work through Jitsi's own screen; we lost the custom controls.

Today I pulled his version into my branch and tested the live site in a browser: the room opens, Join call loads the Jitsi frame with camera, microphone and screen-share permissions set. It was far too small, though. In the narrow video column the frame was about 290 by 165 pixels, and 210 pixels tall on a phone, so I made it at least 420 pixels tall on desktop and 70% of the screen height on phones. I could not test an actual call, because my testing browser blocks the camera. That, and trying it on a real phone, is still to do. Phone browsers generally cannot share a screen, so that button may be missing there.
