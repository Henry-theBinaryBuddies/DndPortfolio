Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

The last two hairstyles. Halflings now start with curls: a fuller cap of hair raised into round knobs, each carved with a groove that spirals into its middle. The first version looked like cobblestones, flat-topped with only a crescent of spiral on each, so Claude rounded the knobs into domes, made the curls a little larger and tightened the spiral, which now makes about a turn and a half. The curls are about 5 mm across on a full-size print, the smallest size that leaves room for a spiral the printer can make. Dwarves now start with a braided beard: the full beard, a little shorter at the chin, with two braids that hang from under the chin and come to rest against the throat and chest, or on armour if it is worn. Each braid is carved as a plait, with a band near the end and a short tuft below it.

Before committing the braids I had Claude run a review of all four new styles with a group of agents, each looking at one area, and a skeptic for each trying to knock down every complaint. It caught real problems. The grooves on the bun were about 0.2 mm wide, half of what the printer can make, and some on the lower ponytail and across the front of the braids were too fine as well. There was a seam in the carving down the back of the head under the ponytail, and the ponytail's tie band could come out as a narrow waist or a bulge depending on the body. All of that is fixed, and a new test measures every groove and ridge on these styles against the printer's minimums. Claude checked that the test really fails on the old bun before trusting it. One thing a skeptic waved off, a dotted look where the ponytail's grooves crowd toward the tie, showed up plainly in the pictures, so it was fixed anyway with fewer, broader grooves. One clothing test timed out while all those agents were running, but passed once the computer was quiet. Everything was committed on the 21st after a clean run of all the tests. None of this model work is merged or online yet.
