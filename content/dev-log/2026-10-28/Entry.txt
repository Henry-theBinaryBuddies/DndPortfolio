Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

From a viewer to something that prints. First more hair: dreadlocks and braids, then an open mouth with teeth, a tongue and expressions, and a jaw that slides forward as it opens, which ended the folds in the throat.

Then the export. It gathers every part of the bust (head, hair, beard, eyes, horns, tusks, teeth) and merges them into one closed solid with manifold-3d, in a background worker so the page does not freeze. A bust needs a base, so it stands on a turned pedestal (a disc with a sloped rim and a waisted stem, like the reference photo of dragon busts) that turns with it. The underside is cut flat where it stops hanging out.

The most useful change: the export refuses to save anything that is not exactly one complete solid, and says what is wrong. I then checked the files with a separate tool, Trimesh, that did not share any of my code. It found places where surfaces touched along an edge, which I fixed. A wall-thickness check found 0.1 to 1.8 percent of the surface thinner than two nozzle widths: ears, lips, nostrils and the tips of hair.
