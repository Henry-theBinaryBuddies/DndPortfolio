Side Quest (Scenic Sorcerer):

I asked Claude to update the portfolio page for Scenic Sorcerer, because the card still showed the early version of the editor, with plain rooms and no art. The old pictures all came from one demo map with no stamps in it, so touching up a caption would not have fixed that. Claude made a new demo map instead, an inn called The Gilded Stag, and photographed the editor on it. The inn has a common room with a long table, a fireplace and a bar, a kitchen, a cellar with a staircase down and a glowing circle on the floor, a round purple wizard's tower with a spiral staircase, a triangular orchard and a yard with a well and a tent. It uses the Tom Cartos props, the stairs and the new floors all at once, so one map shows most of what the editor can do now.

The card has a new cover, which is that map at night with the lighting baked in, and the gallery went from seven pictures to eleven. There is the editor, the stamp panel with Tom Cartos credited at the bottom, a sampler of all seventeen staircases with one spiral selected, a sampler of the floors, the right-click menu, reshaping the triangle, the lighting preview, and a clean exported picture with no grid or GM notes. The levels and hex pictures from before stayed, since they were still true. Every caption and picture description was rewritten to match what is on screen, and the card's tools list now credits the Tom Cartos props.

Claude checked the page by serving a trimmed copy of the site and opening the card in a real browser: the new cover shows, all eleven pictures load, and there were no errors. Its first try was to serve the whole project folder, and a safety check refused that, because it would have exposed everything in the folder, so it copied only the site's own files into a scratch folder and served those. Nothing is online yet. The card still points at an address that does not exist until I set up the Cloudflare Pages project, and I still need to write my own reflection for it.

Artifact 5 (Tome & Tile):

I asked for the weekly report to be refreshed now that John's three pull requests and the renderer packaging had landed, and then for this page's Moonshot card to be brought up to date: a new cover, three pictures and a rewritten About. Taking the pictures turned up a bug. Claude drove the pages with Playwright, the same tool used for the Effigy Engine pictures. That browser has no WebGPU, so the renderer fell back to its WebGL2 path, and on that path the map and the portraits came out blank. The cause was an order of operations. The renderer closed each decoded picture right after handing it to the graphics library, but on that path the copy only happens when the frame is sent, so it copied from a picture that no longer existed. Keeping the picture open until after the frame fixed it. It was the first time the fallback had ever run, and anyone whose browser lacks WebGPU would have seen blank maps. The fix is on its own branch and is not pushed yet.

The first set of pictures also had a mistake in it. The scripted chat said the player had rolled a 21 while the roll card said 20, because the dice are random and the words were not. The lines no longer mention numbers. The card now says what exists today: the server, the renderer, the chat and dice panels, and the mock server. It also says what is still ahead: character sheets, clicking an ability to attack, 3D dice, the video panel, accounts and putting it online.
