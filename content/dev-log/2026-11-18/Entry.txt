Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

Hair day. The eyebrows are finer on everyone now, and each race has its own: elves, half-elves and tieflings get fine, high-arched brows, and dwarves and half-orcs bushy, flat ones. The hairline had a stair-step pattern across the forehead that followed the rows of the head's mesh. Claude traced it to where the hair comes up out of the skin, which followed a sharp edge on the mesh, and made it follow a smoothed edge instead, so the hair now meets the skin along a smooth curve on every race and style. It also tried breaking the edge up with paint so it would fade rather than stop, but that made no visible difference, so it came back out.

Then the first two new hairstyles. The topknot draws the hair up to a bun on the crown, with grooves winding round it. The bun widens only gently from its root, so it should print without supports. The ponytail is tied at the back of the head and falls down the back of the neck. Getting it to lie right took three tries. Falling straight down, it hung in an arc clear of the neck. Leaning in toward the neck from the tie, it pushed into the back of the skull and curled up there. The version that worked falls straight past the skull and only then leans in to rest on the back of the neck, which also means it prints without supports.
