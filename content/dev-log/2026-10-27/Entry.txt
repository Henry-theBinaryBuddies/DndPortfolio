Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

Detail day. Eyes: each eyeball is fitted into its socket so the lids rest on it, with a cornea bulge, working heavy lids and rooted lashes. The mouth: the trenches at the corners were caused by my own added folds, not the sculpt, so I removed the hand-made lip shapes and let the base sculpt's mouth show. A useful habit came out of it: before adding any shaping, compare it against the bare sculpt in grey clay. Half-orc cheeks and chin were fixed the same way, and the tusks now grow out of the mouth.

Hair went from stuck-on strands to sculpted shells with the strands carved into them: cropped, swept, mohawk and a long curtain with face-framing locks. Beards, moustaches and eyebrows followed, each a shell with carved hair.

Then a decision: sculpt only, grey clay, no colour and no character presets until the shape is right. Painted skin made shape problems harder to judge.
