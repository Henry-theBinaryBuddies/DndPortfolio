Side Quest (Effigy Engine):

I told Claude to keep refining: improve the teeth inside the Dragonborn's jaw and polish the other races. I also asked it to ask me what I wanted first, so it would not be working aimlessly. Over two rounds of questions I chose predator teeth, big and uneven, with front teeth, gums, a tongue and bigger fangs. For the races I chose more distinct races, better faces, hair and beards, and a painted look, and then a subtle, realistic touch, a big bulbous nose for the gnome, a signature look for each race, and all four of the hair ideas Claude offered.

The Dragonborn's mouth now has teeth of mixed sizes that curve back toward their tips: large and small in turn down the upper jaw, the lower teeth standing between them, and a pair across the front of the lower jaw. Outside the lips there is a great fang with smaller ones before and behind it, and two more at the tip of the snout, so several show with the mouth shut. There are gum ridges along both jaws and a longer, tapered tongue with a groove down the middle. Two things broke along the way. Opening the jaw wide folded a strip of skin over itself beside the fangs, because the teeth, which stay still, were holding back the skin next to them as it moved. Claude left the teeth and tongue out of the calculation of how the skin follows the jaw, which fixed it. A full scowl also creased the bridge of the snout, and that is fixed too. The 1:12 test file now has two pairs of crossing triangles on the back of the right shoulder, from the random layout of the scales. It does not affect the app's own export, which still merges into one solid, and it is written down in the sculpt's notes.

The races got their signatures. Elves have longer ears, sharper cheekbones and a narrower chin. Halflings have rounder, softer faces, slightly pointed ears and a button nose. Gnomes have the big bulbous nose instead of the long straight one. Dwarves and half-orcs have a heavier brow, a broader nose and a thicker jaw, and the orc a lower forehead. Tieflings are gaunter, with a pointed chin and pointier ears. Two things pushed back. Bigger tiefling ears ran into the ram horn, so they stayed smaller. The longer elf ears reached a little further into dreadlocks than one test allowed, 8.2% of the ear against a limit of 8%, so the limit went up to 9% with the reason written next to it.
