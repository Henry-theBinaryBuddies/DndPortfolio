Side Quest (Adventurer Anvil):

The Details step and a finished Review. The Details step takes the character's name, with suggestions, an alignment grid with a note where the personality leans, appearance with age, height and weight suggested inside the race's range, personality, backstory, allies, notes and a portrait that is shrunk in the browser. The name suggestions come from a seed, so the same character gets the same suggestions every time. Review now says every answer in words with a Change link beside it, says whether the character is ready, and lets a DM allow things the rules would otherwise reject, with a note, and shows the rulings they have made.
