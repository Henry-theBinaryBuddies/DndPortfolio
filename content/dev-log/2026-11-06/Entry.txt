The video call worked for all three of us in a test today: the three of us in one call at once, with a YouTube video playing above it and chat open (photo added to the card). Adrian's camera was off, so he shows as a letter.

A correction to my own story. After my laptop and phone failed to see each other on different networks, I added a relay (our server asks Cloudflare's TURN service for short-lived credentials when you join a call, with the secret key kept on the server). I assumed the cause was two devices on different networks being unable to connect directly. It was actually a firewall on my work network blocking video calls. So the relay was built for the wrong diagnosis. It is merged and harmless, but when I checked the live server today it reported the relay as off (its key was never added on Render), and I have not shown it fixing anything. A call from outside the firewall would be the real check.

Two other things cleared up. The YouTube "sign in to confirm you're not a bot" message was a VPN on one of our connections; turning it off fixed it, so it was never a bug in our code. And I worked out that a 10-person room would use the relay's free 1,000 GB in around 25 hours if everything went through it, and would need about 9 megabits per second of upload from each person, which is why the team capped rooms at 5 people.

Since then I built the extras I had listed: switching mic, camera and speaker during a call without leaving it, a loudness meter on every tile with a ring around whoever is speaking, a mute button and volume slider for each other person (this only changes what you hear), a first-visit tips panel in the room, and a leader-set room name that leaves the link alone. I tested all of it with two browser windows and a fake camera and microphone and pushed it for a teammate's review; it is not merged yet. Not tested: those extras on a phone.

What is left is cosmetic, and John is taking the visual polish.
