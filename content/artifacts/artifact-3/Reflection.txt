This project is the one I'm most proud of so far. It was even more ambitious than the last.
I think I ended up with a product I'll end up using forever.

Main skills developed were primarily administrative and IT skills:
setting up my home server to host the backend for the application, OGL and ORC licensing,
integrating monster sheets for Foundry, JSON and Plutonium compatibility,
AI integration using Claude API, and programming to ensure Claude
tokens remained affordable. Many of the calculations to generate and
build monsters are handled by the backend/business logic, and querying the
AI is primarily for flavor text. This allows me to use Haiku model and
keep costs at a fraction of a penny per generation.

CR calculation for new monsters is done by a statistical averaging of the SRD monsters sourced,
but unfortunately this only has about an 85% accuracy due to the SRD containing
major gaps and outliers in the dataset.
