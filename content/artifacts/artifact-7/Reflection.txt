SIDE QUEST:
Reflection
Another ambitious project. Overall, this was a test in hardware and the limitations of
what kind of 3d blender models codex and Claude code are capable of generating. This wasn't
a perfect success, but it will be something to iteratively improve with time.

The best the graphic quality we could generate based on the slider functionality I wanted to
maintain was circa mid 2000s era character models. Fortunately, the assets can be adapted with
custom-built models, whether they are built by myself or contacted out remains to be decided.
In addition, it is very likely that in sometime the general LLMs will have improved their
skill set in this regard, which may be a faster timeline than trying to work with a graphic designer
or teaching myself blender. Overall, the functionality for Virtual Table Tops is successful and
works for what I will primarily be using this application for, quick PNG images of a custom
character.

The STL 3d prints was always overly ambitious because I have a very legacy 3d printer (Ender3 V1).
With the default 0.4mm nozzle, larger busts prints were successful. However, during
this process I upgraded to a 0.2mm nozzle to maintain finer details at Claude's suggestion. This
was suggested due to the fact the printed busts are far too big to play on a standard sized Dungeons
and Dragons board. Everything started to fall apart at this stage, this was my first time swapping
hardware on my 3d printer, and required a lot of recalibration and testing. While I was able to
successfully calibrate the machine, my machines heat plate and balancing was too archaic to support
the models. The Ender3 V1 is an 8-year-old model, and was never designed to support this level of
detail. To fully test prints, I will need to upgrade the hardware. However, all implemented virtual
tests are successful so theoretically all features are complete.

This was the first project where I set up an agentic workflow with adversarial models. Claude built,
Codex audited and improved sculpting due to having better imaging than Claude. Sometimes I had them
reverse roles to see the difference in improvement, but it ultimately revealed the limitation of using
generated and open-source modeling tools. As stated above, to fully optimize this prototype it will
require either advancement in AI generated blender modeling, or custom-built models.
Fortunately, I ensured that this modular architecture can be supported without code refactors. Future
3d models simply need to maintain:
-A closed, watertight mesh
-Detail sized for printing
-Variants that share topology in place
