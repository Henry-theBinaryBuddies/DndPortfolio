Adventurer Anvil

A character creator for Dungeons & Dragons in the spirit of Roll20's Charactermancer: guided steps, only legal choices, and a summary that shows how every number is worked out. Its first milestone, the builder, is complete: characters of any level from 1 to 20 under the 2014 rules, with SRD 5.1 as the built-in content. It's online at anvil.creaturecauldron.com.

Each step is a page: Rules, Race, Abilities (standard array, point buy, or 4d6 rolled in the app with every die kept in a roll log), Class, Background, Equipment, Spells and Details, then Review and the sheet. Characters can multiclass, with each class's score prerequisites, multiclass proficiencies and shared spell slots, and take Ability Score Improvements or feats, subclasses at their level, Eldritch Invocations, Mystic Arcanum and the rest. The optional rules are switches: feats, rolled hit points, variant encumbrance and moving a race's ability increases. Options you can't take stay visible, greyed out with the reason ("Needs Int 13 (you have Intelligence 10)"), and a DM can allow any rule problem, which stays marked on the character and the sheet.

Characters save themselves in the browser as you go, download as files, open again (with checks against damaged or newer files), and level up later. The inventory keeps changing in play, and the session journal records each game. The sheet shows on screen and prints black on white, and every page works on a phone. Anvil is to be folded into Tome & Tile, the team's virtual tabletop, so play happens there: a character exports as Tome & Tile's own Creature file, built with Tome & Tile's generated contract types, with weapons and spells ready to click.

The core knows nothing about D&D. It knows characters, choices, content packs, calculated values and rule problems. The 2014 rules are a module plugged into it, which also lays out the sheet, so the 2024 rules, Pathfinder or another game can be added later, and a test fails if the core ever mentions a game word. A character file stores only the choices made; everything else is recalculated.

Content comes in JSON packs, checked against a published schema and documented for people writing their own. SRD 5.1 is imported into one from community files: 9 races, 12 classes and subclasses with all 20 levels, 237 items, 319 spells and its one feat. Where the files leave things out, it fills them from the SRD's text (two missing quivers, the Circle of the Land's spells), and every race, class and caster is tested against tables entered by hand. Packs made from books you own will stay private to your account.

Built with TypeScript, React and Vite, with Zod defining the pack format, and served by Cloudflare Pages with a strict Content-Security-Policy: the page loads only its own files and talks to nothing else. 295 unit tests and 50 browser tests, with every page checked for WCAG 2.2 AA at desktop and phone sizes, and the built site checked under its real headers.
