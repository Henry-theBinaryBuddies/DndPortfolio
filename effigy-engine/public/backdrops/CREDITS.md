## Final prompt set

Each image used the shared prompt below followed by `Scene: ` and its scene prompt.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background image for Effigy Engine fantasy character portrait tokens. Generate one square 1024x1024 or larger square image, edge-to-edge opaque artwork. Grim realistic painterly fantasy environment, beautiful restrained atmospheric depth and tactile materials. NO characters, creatures, faces, silhouettes, statues, skulls, writing, logos, border, circle, medallion or frame. A character bust will later cover the middle: central 55% must be dark, quiet, low contrast, diffuse and spacious with no focal object or bright light. Environmental details should be legible around the side shoulders/upper edges within the inner 85%, not concentrated in corners; circular cropping and a rim will hide outermost edges. Overall low-key lighting with moderate subdued accent illumination only at sides, not pure black. Premium cohesive art direction for a dark D&D character creator.
